TermTrek is a heavy-duty engineering simulation featuring a live terminal, a virtual file system, and a Python compiler. It requires a physical keyboard and a larger screen to function properly.

Please open termtrek.dev on your computer.

Privacy & Storage

TermTrek is a zero-tracking environment. We do not use analytical cookies, we do not track your IP, and your code never leaves your browser.

However, to prevent you from losing your progress when you close the tab, we need your permission to save your lesson status locally on your hard drive (using your browser's localStorage).
